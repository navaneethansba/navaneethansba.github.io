HOW TO ADD THE EXECUTIVE INTRODUCTION VIDEO

1. Record/export the final video as MP4 (H.264 video + AAC audio is ideal).
2. Name it exactly: executive-introduction.mp4
3. Place it in this folder: assets/video/
4. Commit/push to GitHub.

The website automatically checks for this file. When it exists, the 90-second Executive Introduction section becomes visible. Until then, that section stays hidden, so there is no broken video or 'coming soon' block on the live site.

Recommended format:
- 1920x1080 or 1280x720
- Landscape 16:9
- 75–90 seconds
- Clean neutral/office background
- Camera at eye level
- Professional lapel or USB microphone
- Avoid reading word-for-word; use the script as a conversational guide
